# Engagement style guide (follow for every new Short and long video)

Based on what worked: Ep 11 "What If You Fell Into a Black Hole?" reached 300 views with a ~78% average watch rate.
It worked because the title is a personal "what if" question, the payoff ("stretched like spaghetti") comes in the
first 10 seconds, and the whole Short is about 60 seconds.

## Titles
- A curiosity question aimed at the viewer: "What If You…?", "Could You Survive…?", "Why You'd Never…", "Why Can't You…?", "Did You Know…?"
- Second person ("you"), concrete, under ~60 characters before the emoji. One emoji, then `#shorts` for Shorts.
- No episode numbers in titles (new viewers don't care which episode it is).

## First 2 seconds (segment 1)
- Never open with "Episode N. Season… Next stop…". Open with the hook question or a startling claim, spoken directly to the viewer.
- Keep it under ~18 words. The on-screen `subtitle` on the title card is the title question itself, so a viewer who sees the first frame reads the question.

## Body
- Put the most visceral or surprising fact in the first 10–15 seconds, then explain.
- Talk to the viewer: "you'd see…", "imagine standing…", "your feet would…".
- Short sentences. One idea per segment. Cut history and dates unless they are the story.
- Shorts: aim for 130–170 words (about 55–70 seconds). Long videos: 8–14 minutes, chaptered, with a question card every chapter.

## Ending
- Open loop: tease tomorrow's stop in one line ("Tomorrow, …").
- Comment prompt + follow: "Which part surprised you most? Tell me in the comments, and follow for tomorrow's stop."
  (Or a topic-specific question such as "Would you go? Tell me in the comments.")

## Descriptions
- First line repeats the title question (it shows in search and under the video).

Note: episodes/ep041–ep127 JSON files were edited directly to follow this guide; the old generator scripts are not the source of truth anymore.

## Thumbnails
- Long videos: full colour, saturated, one clear glowing subject on a dark background, 2–3 huge words on two lines (white line + yellow line), tiny astronaut with an arrow. Never charts, maps, grey or busy images. (`"style": "etched"` in the thumbnail spec brings back the old black-and-white look.)
- Shorts can't have uploaded thumbnails: the opening frame (best image + the question) is the thumbnail, so segment 1 gets the most beautiful image.
- Custom thumbnails only show on YouTube once the channel is verified (youtube.com/verify).
